How to practise

  1. Get ready. Use headphones in a quiet room so the microphone hears only your voice.
  2. Press Start lesson and allow the microphone when your browser asks.
  3. Listen, then sing “aa” on the same note. Any octave is fine. Keep the white needle in the green zone.
  4. Hold it until the bar fills, then the next note plays. Follow the coach hints. Aim for 3 stars.

Tip: in Studio, press Karaoke after separating a song and sing the vocal part yourself.
